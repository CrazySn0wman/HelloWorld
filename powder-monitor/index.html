How the pow score works

Snow from the last two days and the day itself (most weight on overnight), plus bonuses for cold and bluebird skies, minus wind. 7+ = send it, 5+ = worth the drive, 3+ = top-up, below = groomers. Ties go to spots with a bigger freeride reputation.

Who gets the snow

Storms from the NW–N (Nordstau) hit the northern rim: Fieberbrunn, Lofer, Hochkönig, Dachstein. Storms from the S–SW (Südstau) favour the Tauern ridge and Lungau. Cold and calm beats deep and windy.

Before you drop in

This is a weather screen, not an avalanche forecast. Check lawine.at every time, carry beacon, shovel and probe, and ride unknown terrain with a guide.
